# Top 5 Cú Máy Animate Thắng Nhất Trên Kling 2.6 / 3.0

## 1. Orbit Nhẹ Chậm Giữ Vai (Công Thức Thắng 2026 - Khuyên Dùng 90% Trường Hợp)
```text
A slow gentle orbit shot, camera moves smoothly around the subject, keeping the model still and elegant, soft warm light, cinematic vibe, luxury feel
```

## 2. Dolly In Tiến Cận Chi Tiết (Zoom In Chậm)
```text
Slow cinematic dolly in towards the product details, smooth motion, high depth of field, maintaining crisp focus on logo and fabric texture
```

## 3. Pan Slow Ngang Thân Người
```text
Slow horizontal camera pan from left to right, smoothly revealing the full outfit and background, warm ambient lighting
```

## 4. Key Zoom (Sử dụng cho ảnh tĩnh trong CapCut)
- Đặt keyframe zoom 105% -> 115% trong 2.5s.
- Tiết kiệm 30 credit/ảnh.

## 5. Multishot Kling 3.0 Custom
```text
Scene 1 (0-2.5s): close-up of model's face and upper body, gentle smile, soft warm sunlight from window 
Scene 2 (2.5-5s): wider full-body shot, slow orbit camera move around model, showing the outfit detail clearly
```
